cutvelvet

Get started

cutvelvet cuts a short film out of footage you already have. An AI agent of your choosing builds the cut, you watch it and write notes, and it revises.

    Just looking around? The cutvelvet folder includes a finished example with notes on it. Stop this server, then from that folder run cutvelvet serve example. The ? button at the top right explains each tab.
